# PitchSwipe 0.1.0

The first release: PitchSwipe in production at https://noor-api.centralindia.cloudapp.azure.com, with apps for
Android and iOS.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `PitchSwipe-v0.1.0-android.apk` | Android app (Android 7.0 or newer). A debug-signed build that opens the live site. |
| `PitchSwipe-v0.1.0-ios-simulator.zip` | `App.app` for the iOS Simulator. Unzip, then drag it onto a booted simulator. |
| `PitchSwipe-v0.1.0-ios-unsigned.ipa` | iOS device build without a signature. It installs only after signing with an Apple Developer certificate. |

## What's in it

- Public landing page, with sign-up and sign-in (Firebase Authentication): email verification, password reset,
  a profile you can edit, and account deletion.
- Founders: decks that test one thing in 2 or 3 versions, results, who swiped, the results assistant and
  insights. Founder pages need a verified email.
- Swipers: the feed, a practice round, quality checks, demo earnings and launch-reward claims.
- Dark and light themes, the card-character logo, motion and haptics.
- Real accounts and a real MongoDB database; deploys from `main` with health checks and rollback.

## Known limitations

- Swiper earnings are demo earnings; no real money moves.
- The iOS build is unsigned and the Android build is debug-signed: no store releases yet.
- Flagsmith experiments, AI drafting, voice transcription and email need their own keys on the server.
- The terms and privacy notice are drafts that need a legal review.
