# PitchSwipe 1.0.0

The final submission release, from [`swrno/PitchSwipe-main`](https://github.com/swrno/PitchSwipe-main). PitchSwipe runs in
production at https://noor-api.centralindia.cloudapp.azure.com; the apps below open that site.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `PitchSwipe-v1.0.0-android.apk` | Android app (Android 7.0 or newer). A debug-signed build that opens the live site, with voice notes on the phone's own speech service. |
| `PitchSwipe-v1.0.0-ios-simulator.zip` | `App.app` for the iOS Simulator. Unzip, then drag it onto a booted simulator. |
| `PitchSwipe-v1.0.0-ios-unsigned.ipa` | iOS device build without a signature. It installs only after signing with an Apple Developer certificate. |

The apps were built from commit `bb5855b`: Android with Gradle, iOS on GitHub's macOS runner.

## What's new since 0.1.0

### For founders and swipers
- **AI runs on Groq.** Drafting, the Ask assistant, insight wording, comment themes and voice-note translation use a
  free Groq key (main model `openai/gpt-oss-120b`, fallback `openai/gpt-oss-20b`); Claude still works when configured.
- **Flagsmith experiments for real.** Publishing a deck creates and starts its Flagsmith experiment, swipers get
  their version from Flagsmith, exposures and conversions are sent, results are read from Flagsmith, and shipping a
  winner completes the experiment. If Flagsmith refuses, the deck goes live on a labelled local split.
- **Voice notes without Sarvam.** Speech is turned into text on your device (the browser in Chrome, Edge and Safari,
  the phone's speech service in the Android app), and the English translation keeps every figure. In the app no
  audio is kept.
- **Founder feedback fixes.** Switching the test type keeps what you wrote; honest price text when nobody has signed
  up; card translation keeps the card's numbers; a draft's flag name follows its test type.
- **The feed.** Starter decks for the swipe feed, and the feed favours decks that fit the swiper. The 30-card quality
  check is off by default.

### Under the hood
- **CI/CD on this repo:** every merge to `main` runs CI and then deploys to the VM, with health checks and automatic
  rollback.
- **Uptime check** every 30 minutes, and an **encrypted off-site copy** of the nightly database backup every day.
- **Repository layout:** the Capacitor apps are in `mobile/`, the VM files in `infra/`, and the spec, designs and
  marketing in `docs/`.
- A MongoDB integration test for the store code, and no more Mongoose deprecation warnings.

## Known limitations

- Swiper earnings are demo earnings; no real money moves.
- The Android build is debug-signed and the iOS build is unsigned: no store releases yet.
- Voice notes are not in the iOS app yet: the speech plugin has no Swift Package Manager support (see
  `docs/mobile.md`).
- Email needs a Resend key and a verified sending domain.
- The terms and privacy notice are drafts that need a legal review.
